Hi, I'm Joe, and Today i'll talk & Explain about [Pwn Sift](https://pwnsift.github.io) but before we jump into the `Core Problem` i'll talking about `Why i made this` in short i made this, lets call `Pwn Sift` for my Blog, Project and Experimentation as an Example NetHydra.

> Q: but why NetHydra redirecting **You** to Pwn Sift?

This is the main problem: I am developing NetHydra for my own business in my room (I prefer to call it the Lab Room), for example for experimental purposes and so on. However, I am facing serious problems with NetHydra.

## BlackTrack and NetHydra

Back in 2023 I am building **BlackTrack** not **BackTrack** which **BlackTrack** itself was inspired By [Kali Linux](https://kali.org/). But why i am not using [Kali](https://kali.org/) instead?

Well, I grew up with [Kali](https://kali.org/),  especially **Legacy Kali** which is 2014-Early 2019 then The problem began **Modern Kali** `!=` with **Old Kali** There is also OffSec (formerly Offensive Security), which underwent a CEO change that shaped the company into what it is today—a shift that also impacted Kali Linux itself. Anyway, that’s enough; I don’t want to discuss this any further. After all, this is merely my own opinion and how I feel—and, of course, everyone has their own views and opinions.

and that's why i build BlackTrack. Long story short, let's say we [rebranding BlackTrack to HydraPWK](https://nethydra.github.io/blog/hydrapwk-2025.1.6.5-release).This is where the new problem began to arise: the direction of the project—originally intended to let me build my own version of an "old-school Kali Linux"—slowly shifted without me even realizing it.

Then continue with NetHydra—And I just realized that many of the decisions I made strayed significantly from the original goal. What I initially built out of what I could call 'love'—born of necessity—turned into an 'obligation,' and that has left me exhausted.

And that is why I founded Pwn Sift.

It is neither an organization nor a community, but rather a space for every project and experiment I create—without any sense of obligation.
A plus side is that I will likely conduct a lot of experiments.
However, the downside is that development might be slightly slower. This applies to NetHydra as well; there will be many experiments incorporated into it moving forward.

But what I like most is ___"I build my project exactly the way I want."___

I’ll also be more active here; suffice it to say that all projects are being moved to Pwn Sift—for instance, it is only fitting that NetHydra publishes its releases here.

## Conclusion

`Pwn Sift` is maintained by me ([@me-joe](https://github.com/me-joe)) for every my project i made (Develop). `Pwn Sift` is **NOT** organization nor Community project.
